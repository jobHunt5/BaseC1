Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated 2026-07-19. This has not been reviewed by a lawyer; treat it as a starting point, not a finished legal document.

What we store about you

  • Account: your email address and a salted, hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Profile: whatever you choose to fill in — name, city, phone, industries, work preferences, education, experience.
  • Your activity in the app: areas you've scanned, companies you've saved/rated/added notes to, and which jobs you've marked as applied.
  • Outreach email account (optional): if you connect your own email account to send outreach, the app password/token you provide is encrypted at rest and is only ever used to send email through your own account — it is never stored or shown in plain text after you save it.

What we don't store

We don't store your email account's actual password in plain text, and we don't store payment details (AreaHunt doesn't handle payments).

Company data

Company names, websites, careers contacts, and job listings shown in the app come from a places data provider and from automatically visiting each company's own public website — this is public business information, not personal data about you.

Third parties we send data to

  • A places data provider (Google Places, or OpenStreetMap), to find businesses in the area you draw.
  • Serper.dev, to search external job boards and verify LinkedIn company pages, if configured.
  • OpenAI, only if AI-assisted cover letters or company-fit scoring is enabled — the company description used is public information already shown in the app, not your personal data.
  • Your own email provider, only if you connect an account for outreach — we don't use a third-party shared mail relay.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your profile or activity with other users.

Cookies / local storage

AreaHunt stores your login session token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It isn't used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, saved companies, notes, outreach account settings) at any time from Profile → Delete account.

Admin access

A small number of administrators can view account status and, if necessary, suspend or delete an account (for example, to act on an abuse report). Admin access is logged and password-protected separately from regular accounts.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed in your account/admin settings.
