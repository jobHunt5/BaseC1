What AreaHunt does

AreaHunt lets you draw an area on a map, discovers businesses in that area from a places data provider, and attempts to find each business's careers page, a careers contact, and public job listings by visiting their public websites. You review and act on this information yourself — AreaHunt never applies to a job or contacts a company on your behalf without you explicitly choosing to send that message.
